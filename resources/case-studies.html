Case Studies · Coming Soon

Real businesses. Measurable finance outcomes.

We are preparing practical, anonymised case studies that show the finance problem, what changed, and the business outcome — without marketing fluff or inflated claims.

What you will find here

Each case study will focus on a real finance problem faced by a promoter-led business and the practical work used to improve it.

01

Cash & working capital

Debtor ageing, inventory holding, creditor terms and the changes that improve cash conversion without slowing the business.

02

MIS & decision-making

How management reporting moves from delayed accounting data to a monthly view that promoters can actually use for decisions.

03

Margins & profitability

Product, customer and channel profitability work that helps a business see where margin is being made, lost or quietly diluted.

How each case study will be structured

The emphasis will stay on the business situation, the finance work and the measurable result — not on a long sales story.

01

Business context

The size and nature of the business, how finance was operating and what the promoter was trying to solve.

02

The finance gap

The specific issue — cash, reporting, margin, controls, funding readiness or another decision-critical finance problem.

03

What changed

The reports, processes, reviews, controls or management routines that were put in place with the business team.

04

The outcome

What improved in the numbers, visibility or decision-making, with clear context around what the result actually means.

In preparation

Our first detailed case studies are being prepared.

We are documenting the work carefully and anonymising client information before publishing. Until then, you can explore our practical resources or speak with us about the finance problem you are trying to solve.
