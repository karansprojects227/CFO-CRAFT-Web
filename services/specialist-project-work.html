Where we work

Every project starts from a specific trigger and ends with a defined deliverable. If the need turns out to be ongoing, we will say so and suggest the right retainer instead.

Cash flow and working capital

When you would call us

  • Cash is tight despite reported profit
  • Debtor days or inventory are rising
  • Bank limits are fully drawn

Typical scope

  • 13-week cash flow forecast
  • Debtor, creditor and inventory review
  • Working-capital release plan with owners
  • Review of limit utilisation and structure

Compliance and controls

When you would call us

  • Reconciliation backlogs have built up
  • Controls haven't kept pace with growth
  • An audit has raised control observations

Typical scope

  • Compliance health review across GST, TDS and ROC
  • Clean-up of reconciliation backlogs
  • Internal control and approval design
  • Finance SOPs and delegation of authority

Fundraising and banking

When you would call us

  • A new plant or capex needs a term loan
  • Working-capital limits need enhancement
  • Refinancing, or preparing for an equity raise

Typical scope

  • Financial projections and business plan
  • CMA data and lender information pack
  • Lender engagement and query management
  • Investor-ready financial information, where relevant

ERP and finance transformation

When you would call us

  • Finance runs on spreadsheets around the ledger
  • You are moving to, or struggling with, an ERP
  • Growth has outpaced your systems

Typical scope

  • Finance requirements and process design
  • Vendor evaluation support
  • Chart of accounts and reporting design
  • Data migration oversight, testing and post go-live stabilisation

M&A and integration

When you would call us

  • You are buying, selling or merging a business
  • A promoter restructuring is planned
  • An acquired business needs to be integrated

Typical scope

  • Financial due diligence
  • Coordination of valuation, with a registered valuer where the law requires one
  • Transaction financial modelling
  • Post-acquisition integration of the finance function

How a project runs

Fixed scope, stated deliverables and a clear end point.

  1. Scoping call

    We understand the trigger, the stakes and the timeline, and confirm whether a project is the right route.

  2. Written proposal

    Scope, deliverables, timeline, fee and your team's inputs, in writing, before any work begins.

  3. Engagement letter

    The signed letter of engagement governs the project.

  4. Delivery with milestone check-ins

    Progress is reviewed against the agreed milestones, and scope changes are agreed in writing.

  5. Handover

    Deliverables, working files and next steps are handed to your team.

Good to know

  • Projects can run on their own or alongside a Starter, Core or Growth retainer.
  • Statutory audit, attest work and any statutorily required valuation are carried out by independent, appropriately registered professionals.
  • We don't guarantee transaction outcomes such as loan sanction, valuation or deal completion. We make sure your side of the process is prepared and well run.
