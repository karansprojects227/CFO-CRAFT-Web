FAQs

Questions promoters ask us

What exactly is a Virtual / Fractional CFO?

A senior CFO who leads your finance function part-time, on a monthly retainer — the strategic financial leadership a large company gets from a full-time CFO, sized and priced for a growing business. You get the judgment, the reporting rhythm and the accountability, without the cost or hiring risk of a full-time hire.

How is this different from my current CA or accountant?

Your accountant records what has already happened and keeps you compliant — essential work. A CFO looks forward: what the numbers mean, what's coming, and what to do about it. We work alongside your existing CA, not instead of them — we turn their books into decisions.

Which businesses do you work best with?

Growing manufacturing and trading businesses, typically in the ₹20–200 crore revenue band, where cash and margin discipline decide who wins. We also support professional services firms and highgrowth startups. If you're not sure you fit, a discovery call will tell us both quickly.

Do I need to change my accounting software?

No. We work with what you already run — Tally, Zoho and others. If a change would genuinely help, we'll say so and guide the transition — but it's never a precondition to working with us.

How do we get started?

With a short discovery call to understand your business and where you stand. From there, most engagements begin with a fixed-fee Finance Diagnostic — an honest picture of your finance function — before any longer-term commitment.

How do you charge?

Fixed and retainer-based, never hourly. The Diagnostic is a one-time fixed fee; ongoing work is a predictable monthly retainer scoped to your business. You pay for outcomes and ownership, not hours on a clock.

Is my financial information confidential?

Entirely. We handle your financials under strict confidentiality, with access controls and clear datahandling practices. Your numbers are never shared, and we're happy to sign an NDA before you share anything sensitive.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Primarily remote, with on-site visits where they add value — onboarding, key reviews or specific projects. Our reporting rhythm and monthly advisory run smoothly wherever you're based.
